Travel and Expense Policy

Booking Business Travel
Travel should be booked through the company travel tool wherever possible, because it captures the negotiated rates and gives the finance team visibility of committed spend. Booking outside the tool is permitted when it is materially cheaper, but the difference should be noted on the expense claim.

Advance booking matters more than cabin class. A flight booked three weeks ahead in economy is routinely cheaper than one booked two days ahead, and the saving is larger than any policy on seat selection.

Meals And Subsistence
Reasonable meal costs are reimbursed while travelling on business. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Where a meal is already provided by a conference or a client, it should not also be claimed.

Receipts are required for any single item over twenty five pounds. Below that, a card statement line is sufficient, which keeps the administrative cost of the policy below the value it protects.

Approval And Submission
Expenses should be submitted within thirty days of being incurred. Claims older than ninety days require director approval, because the accounting period they belong to has usually closed by then.

A manager approving a claim is confirming that the spend was necessary and reasonable, not merely that the arithmetic is right.
